BEHIND THE CUP

Made slowly. Shared freely.

NOVA started with one tiny roaster, a second-hand espresso machine, and an unreasonable obsession with getting coffee just right.

Today, we still roast in small batches, know our regulars by name, and believe the best cafés should feel less like businesses and more like places you belong.
